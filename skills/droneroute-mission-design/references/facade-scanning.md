# Facade Scanning

## Purpose

Capture vertical surfaces.

Suitable for:

- Buildings
- Bridges
- Towers
- Monuments

---

## Recommended Strategy

Maintain:

- Constant distance
- Uniform speed
- Consistent lighting

Prefer:

- Multiple altitude bands
- Oblique imagery

---

## Common Risks

- Reflective glass
- Moving shadows
- Strong perspective distortion

---

## Best Practices

Combine facade scans with:

- Orbit missions
- Roof coverage
- Nadir imagery

for complete reconstruction.
