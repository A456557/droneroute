# Photogrammetry Patterns

## Grid Survey

Recommended for:

- Orthophotos
- DSM
- DTM
- Mapping

Typical settings:

- Front overlap: 80%
- Side overlap: 70%

Advantages:

- Fast
- Predictable
- Efficient

Limitations:

- Weak facade coverage
- Limited reconstruction quality

---

## Double Grid

Recommended for:

- Digital twins
- Complex terrain
- Engineering projects

Typical settings:

- Front overlap: 85%
- Side overlap: 80%

Advantages:

- Better 3D geometry
- Strong reconstruction quality

Limitations:

- Longer flight duration
- More images

---

## Corridor Survey

Recommended for:

- Roads
- Railways
- Pipelines
- Utilities

Advantages:

- Optimized flight path
- Reduced battery usage

Limitations:

- Less efficient for large polygons
